1. Getting started

You need an internet connection, a browser, an email address you can open and a GCash account for online payments. Keep your booking reference and payment reference; these are different numbers.

Booking schedules and payment receipt dates use Philippine time (Asia/Manila). Prices may change for new bookings; use the amount displayed in your booking summary. Your saved reservation retains its booked package rates and cancellation/refund policy.

Quick start: Create an account → confirm your email → sign in → select dates → fill in guest details → save the booking → pay and upload your original GCash screenshot → wait for staff verification → check Manage Booking.

2. Create and confirm your account

  1. Open Create Account.
  2. Enter the required personal and contact information. Use an email address you can access.
  3. Enter your password and repeat it in Confirm Password. Follow the form's password requirements.
  4. Submit the registration form.
  5. Open the resort's confirmation email. Check Spam or Junk if necessary.
  6. Follow the confirmation link in the newest email.
  7. Go to the sign-in page and sign in normally. Confirming an email does not automatically sign you in.

Expected result: Your email is confirmed and you can access your guest account. Correct login credentials on an unconfirmed account produce Please confirm your email first.

If the email does not arrive, use Resend confirmation email on the registration result screen. Wait at least one minute between requests and use the newest link. If the link is invalid or expired, request a new one. Ask resort support for help if delivery continues to fail.

3. Sign in, recover access and update your profile

Sign in and sign out

  1. Open the guest sign-in page.
  2. Enter your registered email and password, then submit.
  3. Use the account menu to open your profile or sign out when finished.

Only one device session is active per account. Signing in on another device ends the previous session; sign in again on the device you intend to use. Separate guests should use their own accounts.

If your account has been deactivated, sign-in and protected booking actions are blocked. Contact the resort for help. After staff restore access, sign in again. Existing reservation, payment and refund records remain with the resort.

Forgot your password?

  1. Select Forgot password? on the sign-in page.
  2. Enter your registered email and submit the request.
  3. Follow the recovery instructions in the email.
  4. Set your new password, then return to sign in.

Update your profile

  1. Open Profile Settings from the account menu.
  2. Update the editable name, phone and address fields.
  3. Select Save Changes and wait for the result.

The email field is read-only on this screen. Contact the resort if you need help with an account email change. Changing your profile does not change a reservation's dates, selected services or bill.

4. Book a resort stay

Step A — Choose a date and package

  1. Open Book Now and choose Full Stay Booking.
  2. Select a booking mode and an available date in the calendar.
  3. For Whole Day, choose the required variant. For a custom schedule, fill in the available custom date/time fields and follow the validation messages.
  4. Review the displayed check-in, check-out and package rate.
  5. Select Continue to Reservation.
PackageFixed schedule in Philippine time
Day8:00 AM–4:00 PM
Night6:00 PM–6:00 AM the next day
Whole Day — Variant A8:00 AM–6:00 AM the next day
Whole Day — Variant B6:00 PM–4:00 PM the next day

Guest bookings must start at least 30 minutes from the current time. Dates or package starts may be unavailable because of another booking, a temporary unpaid hold or maintenance. Availability is checked again when saving; selecting a date does not reserve it.

Weekday prices apply Monday–Thursday and weekend prices Friday–Sunday, based on the start date. The standard packages include 20 guests, with additional-person charges above that count and a maximum of 30 guests. Follow any additional unit/form limits shown. Automatic holiday price overrides are not currently included.

Step B — Enter guest details and services

  1. Check your personal and contact information.
  2. Enter the number of adults and children accurately.
  3. Select a Room Type from the available options.
  4. Choose any additional services and quantities offered by the form.
  5. Enter special requests, if needed.
  6. Check Billing Summary, then select Review Full Details.

Step C — Review and save

  1. Review the guest information, dates, package, room, guest counts, selected services and bill.
  2. Use Back to Edit if a detail is wrong.
  3. Select Save Booking once the details are correct.
  4. Keep the generated booking reference and note the displayed payment deadline.
  5. Select Continue to Payment.

Expected result: The booking is saved with a temporary hold. Saving alone does not confirm a paid reservation. A new unpaid hold normally lasts 30 minutes; submit valid payment proof before the displayed deadline. Payment submission then waits for staff review.

If prices changed while you were filling in the form, return to the booking form and review the refreshed total before saving. If saving reports an unavailable date, choose another available date/package.

5. Pay through GCash

Online payment submissions use GCash. If you pay an outstanding balance in cash at the resort, staff will record the received cash and issue a receipt. Check your updated balance in Manage Booking afterward.

Step A — Select what to pay

  1. Confirm that your booking reference and total are correct.
  2. Select GCash and the available provider/account.
  3. For your first payment, choose the displayed minimum Down Payment or Full Payment. The current minimum down payment is 20% of the reservation total.
  4. After a verified payment, use Full Balance for the outstanding amount.
  5. Review Amount due now, Already paid and the balance preview.

Step B — Send the payment

  1. Use the displayed resort QR code and recipient details. Use its image link if you need a larger view.
  2. Open GCash and use the displayed QR/account to send payment.
  3. Confirm the recipient and amount in GCash before completing the transfer.
  4. Save an original screenshot taken directly in GCash of the successful transaction.

If no usable QR/account is available, select Reload payment options. Contact the resort if it remains unavailable; do not send money to an account guessed from an old image or brochure.

Step C — Submit the proof

  1. Enter Amount sent (PHP) exactly as shown on the GCash receipt.
  2. Fill in Account Name, Your Account Number and Reference Number.
  3. Upload your original screenshot under Upload original GCash screenshot. Check the preview; replace or remove an incorrect file.
  4. Read the booking agreement, terms, privacy and cancellation/refund information. Confirm the required agreement.
  5. Select Confirm Payment once and wait for the result.
  6. On Payment Submitted!, keep your booking reference and open Manage Booking to monitor the review.

Receipt requirements

  • Upload PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 8 MB. Use the original, full-size screenshot from your gallery.
  • Keep the successful transaction status, amount sent, recipient, full reference number and payment date/time visible and readable.
  • The receipt date must be today in Philippine time, including for balance payments.
  • The amount and reference you enter must match the screenshot. The reference must be a complete supported numeric reference, not a phone number; do not remove leading zeros.
  • The amount must meet the selected minimum. A fee or total including fees cannot replace the actual amount sent.
  • Photos of another phone's screen, scans, blurred images and edited receipts are not accepted. Do not crop away required details.
  • A previously used payment reference cannot be reused for another payment.

The website checks receipt readability and consistency. Staff must still verify that the money arrived. A successful upload is not payment approval. Do not submit a second payment while a payment is awaiting review.

Paying more than requested

Larger payments are recorded at their actual verified amount. For example, with a PHP 5,000 bill and PHP 1,000 minimum down payment:

Amount actually sentResult after staff approval
PHP 800Below the minimum; cannot be submitted as the required down payment
PHP 1,500PHP 1,500 is credited; PHP 3,500 remains payable
PHP 5,000The bill is fully paid
PHP 5,500The bill is fully paid and PHP 500 is excess; request its refund in Manage Booking

These are examples, not the resort's package prices. Active-booking excess refunds need a request; eligible cancellation refunds are created automatically. All payouts require manual staff processing and verification.

6. View your booking and pay the balance

  1. Sign in with the account used for the booking.
  2. Open Manage Booking and select the Bookings tab.
  3. Find your booking reference, open its three-dot options menu, and select View. Details open in a dialog over the records.
  4. Check the schedule, status, booked policy, services and remaining balance.
  5. When payment is available, use Pay balance in the options menu or Continue to Payment in the details dialog.
  6. Complete the GCash payment and proof steps using today's original receipt.

You can search by booking reference. Close a dialog with Close, its top-right close icon, or Escape. The page stays in place while the dialog is open; saving temporarily disables closing. Unavailable menu options explain the restriction.

Expected result: Staff approval credits the actual amount received and updates the balance. Your reservation can be Confirmed after a verified down payment and still have a balance to pay.

If resort staff entered your booking through Messenger, a phone call or a walk-in, it may be linked to a staff-created walk-in profile rather than your website account. Ask staff to identify the record if it does not appear in your account; do not create another booking to replace it without checking.

7. Add services to a booking

  1. Open Manage Booking and open the reservation's three-dot options menu and select Edit services.
  2. In Edit Booking Services, update the quantities allowed by the form. Existing booked quantities have a displayed minimum.
  3. Under Add New Service, select an available service and quantity, then select Add Service.
  4. Select Save Service Changes.
  5. After saving, the dialog shows the updated bill, outstanding balance and confirmation message. Pay additional charges through the payment flow when available.

Expected result: The selected services are saved and billing is recalculated. The guest Edit screen edits services; it does not change the reservation package, accommodation, guest count or contact details. Contact staff for those reservation changes.

Closed reservations and bookings under payment review cannot be edited through this screen. If the form refuses a change, follow its message or contact staff rather than creating a duplicate reservation.

8. Request a different booking date

  1. Find a Confirmed reservation in Manage Booking.
  2. Open the three-dot options menu and select Reschedule. Choose your date in the dialog.
  3. Use Previous month or Next month in the custom calendar, select a new check-in date, and review the retained package time/duration. Past dates, dates too early for the original start time, and the current reservation date are disabled. Availability is checked when you submit.
  4. Submit the request and read the notice showing the additional charge if approved.
  5. Wait for staff review and check the result in Manage Booking.
  6. If approved, check the new schedule and use Pay Balance for additional charges when required.

Expected result: A pending request does not immediately move the reservation. The original dates remain reserved while staff reviews it. Approval can add a notice fee and a higher-date-rate adjustment; moving to a cheaper date does not automatically refund the difference.

Rescheduling requires a confirmed payment, an eligible available date and the applicable booking rules. The new start must remain at least 30 minutes away. Contact staff if Reschedule is disabled or your requested change is outside the available options.

The original swimming date requires at least 14 Philippine calendar days' notice for March-May and September-December, or 7 days for other months. The dialog displays your deadline and notice fee. Late requests are disabled; contact the resort to ask about a staff-reviewed exception. Your package, original time and duration stay fixed.

Notice fees are PHP 50 for 6+ days, PHP 100 for 3-5 days, PHP 300 for 1-2 days, and PHP 500 on or after the original date. Short-notice changes require an authorized staff exception. On-time requests carry PHP 50 plus any higher-date-rate adjustment when approved. Your existing payments remain on the bill.

9. Cancel a booking

  1. Open Manage Booking and open the reservation's three-dot options menu and select Cancel reservation.
  2. Review the server-calculated preview: verified payments, previous refunds, initial deposit basis, retained deposit and refundable amount.
  3. Check the acknowledgement and select Confirm cancellation. If the bill or payments changed, reload the preview and review it again.
  4. Read the success alert and updated booking status. Eligible cancellations create a refund request automatically; you do not submit another request.
  5. Open the Refunds tab for payment review, administrator approval, manual transfer and staff verification.

Guest cancellation retains the initial 20% deposit. A downpayment-only booking has no refund; a fully paid booking receives the remaining 80%. Partial payments above the required deposit can be refunded after deducting that deposit and prior completed refunds. No payment means no request. A documented cancellation attributable to the resort refunds all remaining verified payments, including the deposit.

Expected result: The reservation becomes Cancelled and releases its dates. It is shown as Cancelled - No Refund when nothing is refundable; refund processing otherwise proceeds separately. No money is transferred automatically. Refunds are sent manually through GCash or another approved method and become Refunded only after authorized staff verify the transfer.

Guest cancellation retains the booked notice period. Pending payment proofs must be reviewed before cancellation. Closed/completed bookings cannot be cancelled again. Contact staff if self-service is unavailable. Read in-app alerts and email updates; email delivery issues do not undo a saved cancellation.

10. Request an excess-payment refund

  1. Wait until staff has verified the payment.
  2. Open Manage Booking and select the Refunds tab. You can also select View refunds in a booking's details dialog to open the tab filtered to that booking reference. Clear the reference search to see all requests.
  3. Check the eligible booking reference and displayed excess amount.
  4. Select Request excess refund once.
  5. Monitor the request: Pending means awaiting review; Approved means awaiting GCash payout; Transfer submitted means evidence is awaiting staff verification; Refunded means the transfer was verified.
  6. Read any review note and retain the GCash payout reference when shown.

Expected result: The resort reviews the calculated excess. This request does not cancel your booking. The system calculates eligibility from the current bill, verified payments and completed refunds; you do not type a refund amount.

An open refund, a payment awaiting review or a pending reschedule can block another excess-refund request. Adding services may reduce or remove an excess before payout. Contact staff if a recorded payout is not visible in your GCash account.

11. Schedule and manage an ocular visit

An ocular visit is a resort viewing, not a paid stay reservation.

Schedule through the working booking flow

  1. Open Book Now at /booking and choose Ocular Visit.
  2. Select an available date and time slot.
  3. Sign in if requested.
  4. Select Schedule Visit and wait for the saved result.
  5. Open Manage Booking, select the Ocular visits tab and confirm the visit appears.
  6. Wait for staff approval before treating the visit as confirmed.

Visits are free and require approval. Slots starting in less than 30 minutes are unavailable.

View, change or cancel

  1. Open the Ocular visits tab in Manage Booking.
  2. Open the visit's three-dot options menu and select View to inspect it in a dialog.
  3. Use Edit visit in the same menu to choose an available date/slot in the dialog and save the change. Check the updated status and await approval when required.
  4. Use Cancel visit in the menu and confirm if you no longer need the visit.

Current screen note: The separate /ocular page is an older demonstration screen that displays a local success message without saving a visit. Use Book Now → Ocular Visit and verify the saved record in Manage Booking, including if a link takes you to the older page.

12. Write a review with a photo

  1. Sign in to your registered guest account and open Reviews after your visit.
  2. Select Write a Review.
  3. Choose your Overall Rating, and fill in any other rating categories you want to provide.
  4. Enter a Review Title and Your Review. Write at least 50 characters.
  5. Optionally select one photo under Add a photo (optional). Use JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 3 MB.
  6. Check the photo preview; replace it or use Remove photo if needed.
  7. Optionally enter a reservation ID and select the recommendation checkbox.
  8. Submit and wait for the success message.

Expected result: Your review is saved. The current system automatically publishes newly submitted reviews; public photos are shown for approved reviews. Use View full photo on a review to open its image.

The system currently permits one review per guest account. There is no guest review-editing screen. Contact the resort if your submitted review needs correction. Avoid including private account or payment information in public text or photos.

3. Daily operating checklist

At the start of a shift:

  1. Open Dashboard for today's arrivals, verified collections and unresolved requests. Open Reservations to review the calendar and maintenance blocks.
  2. Open Transactions → Payment Verification Queue and reconcile pending proofs with the resort's receiving account.
  3. Open Records → Reschedule Requests and Ocular Visit Records for pending decisions.
  4. Open Transactions → Refund Requests for requests awaiting review or payout.
  5. Check balances for arriving guests and record money received through the correct payment workflow.

During a stay, record added services through the reservation editor and check the resulting payable amount. Before leaving, use Reports for date-based collection/activity totals, inspect unresolved items and sign out.

Read and use the live dashboard

The dashboard reads saved records and refreshes every minute while the page is visible, when you return to the window, and when you select Refresh dashboard or the arrivals table's Refresh. The last successful update is displayed in Philippine time. A failed refresh shows an error and identifies the older data; do not treat it as a successful update.

  • Arrivals today: Active reservations starting on today's Philippine calendar date, including payment holds that are still valid.
  • Scheduled now: Confirmed/reschedule-requested bookings within their scheduled stay times. This is a schedule count, not evidence that the guests have checked in.
  • Collections today: Verified payments dated today, before refunds. Verification date is used; older records without it use creation date.
  • Bookings created: All reservations created in the last seven days, including today and cancelled/expired bookings.

Needs attention shows all unresolved payment proofs, reschedules, ocular requests, refund reviews and approved transfers or submitted payouts awaiting verification, including older requests. Select a queue card to open the corresponding tab in Transactions or Records. Links only appear for modules your role can access; permission to view a queue does not grant permission to approve its records.

The Last 7 days charts show verified collections and the current recorded status/source of bookings created during those dates. Use Booking status/Booking source to change the breakdown, and each View dropdown to select a line graph, bar chart, pie chart or progress bars. Staff entry includes phone, Messenger and walk-in reservations.

In Arrivals & stays, select Today, Next 7 days, Next 30 days or Scheduled now. Arrival periods include today. Closed reservations and expired payment holds are excluded. Search by guest/reference, filter status/source, sort columns and use the paging controls. Export CSV downloads all matching rows across pages. Table filters do not change the cards or charts. Export is disabled when there are no matching rows, while refreshing, or after a failed refresh.

Select a row's View to open that reservation directly in the calendar editor. + New reservation opens the staff booking dialog when your account has both View reservations and Add reservation permissions. The calendar, Reports and Analytics shortcuts open their respective modules. Use the calendar for reservations beyond the next 30 days.

4. Find a reservation in the calendar

  1. Open Reservations.
  2. Navigate to the required month using the calendar controls.
  3. Use the search icon and Search calendar to look up a reference or guest name within the loaded calendar period.
  4. Select the booking event or its date marker to open the reservation dialog.
  5. Read its schedule, guest details, services, bill and any read-only explanation.
  6. Select Close when finished, or follow the editing steps below.

Calendar indicators distinguish reservations, items needing attention, ocular visits and maintenance. A date selected in the calendar is not proof that a bill is paid; check the verified payments and transaction balance.

5. Enter a phone, Messenger or walk-in reservation

Required permission: Add reservation.

  1. Open Reservations and select + New Reservation.
  2. In New Manual Reservation, select Guest type:
    • Existing account: Select the correct registered guest.
    • Walk-in guest: Enter the first name, last name, email, phone and address required by the form. This option also supports people booking by phone or Messenger without a website account.
  3. Click a selectable day in the custom Booking date calendar. Use the arrows to change months. Booked dates, maintenance closures and past dates are disabled. Day only or Night only means the other start is unavailable. Choose an available Booking package and, if applicable, Whole-day variant. Overnight and whole-day availability includes the following day. Fixed start times must be at least 30 minutes from now in Philippine time.
  4. Select the Unit, and enter the adults and children counts.
  5. Under Additional services, check the services requested and enter a whole-number quantity for each. Add special requests, including the booking channel or relevant guest instructions when helpful.
  6. Review the package, extra guests, service charges, total and minimum downpayment in Billing preview. Availability refreshes automatically and is checked again by the server when saving. Use Retry availability if loading fails; creating a reservation stays blocked until availability loads.
  7. Select Create Manual Reservation and wait for the calendar to reload.
  8. Find the new reference in the calendar and Transactions. Record any money actually received through the appropriate payment workflow promptly.

Expected result: A reservation, guest association and initial bill are recorded. The entry starts unpaid/pending; creating a manual booking does not itself record money or issue a payment receipt. Observe its payment hold/deadline and current status.

Selected services and quantities are attached to the reservation using current catalog prices. Open the saved reservation editor to add or update services later. A walk-in profile is separate from a registered website account, so its reservation may not appear in a guest's personal Manage Booking page.

If a save reports that a booking was created but a follow-up action failed, look up the reference before retrying. Avoid duplicating the booking.

6. Edit reservation details and add services

Required permission: Edit reservations and services.

Before arrival

  1. Select the reservation in Reservations.
  2. Review the original schedule and Updated bill preview.
  3. Use the custom Arrival date calendar to choose a date available for the Booked package. The booked package, whole-day variant, original start time and duration cannot be changed. A date stays disabled when that package conflicts, even if another package has a free slot. The reservation being edited is excluded from conflicts. Existing custom schedules also retain their original times and duration when moved. Change guest counts or accommodation if needed.
  4. Adjust service selections/quantities and special requests as required.
  5. Review Previous total, Package adjustment, Reschedule notice fee, Services total, New total, Already paid, Amount payable and any Overpayment. A service-only edit has no reschedule notice fee.
  6. Enter a specific Reason for change, such as “Guest requested a date change through Messenger.”
  7. Select Save reservation.
  8. Check the success result, refreshed calendar and saved bill. Open View transactions and receipts if you need billing records.

During a stay

  1. Open the active reservation in the same way.
  2. Add available services or increase existing quantities. Update notes/special requests as needed.
  3. Review the additional charge and amount payable.
  4. Enter the reason and select Save reservation.
  5. Collect and record additional payment through Transactions when required.

Expected result: Reservation details and the current bill/invoice update together, with a recorded reason. Existing payments and issued receipts keep their original amounts.

Once the stay starts, schedule/details changes are restricted and previously booked services cannot be removed or reduced. Ended, closed or expired bookings and bookings awaiting payment/reschedule review can be read-only. An unavailable date or a concurrent change can also stop saving; reload and inspect the current record before trying again.

Reschedule notice: The original swimming date requires at least 14 Philippine calendar days' notice in March-May and September-December, or 7 days in other months. The editor displays the deadline. Notice fees are PHP 50 for 6+ days, PHP 100 for 3-5 days, PHP 300 for 1-2 days, and PHP 500 on or after the original date. Ordinary on-time reschedules still carry PHP 50.

Late exceptions: Staff need both Edit reservation and Reschedule approval permissions. For a confirmed reservation, check the late-exception acknowledgement, choose an available future date, review the charge, and document the guest request in Reason for change. A still-confirmed reservation with a past schedule may be moved; completed/cancelled bookings cannot be reopened. Existing accommodation and guest counts remain locked after the original arrival. Resolve pending reviews/open refunds first. Date, bill, invoice and audit changes save together; a linked guest receives an in-app notification when delivery succeeds. Apply docs/reschedule-policy-migration.sql before using this feature.

Existing reservations use their booked package-rate schedule for package differences. Existing services retain their booked prices; newly added services use their available catalog prices. A later Catalog price change does not reprice all existing bookings. Changing accommodation still follows package-based billing; inspect the preview rather than assuming a unit rate replaces the package bill.

Reservation editing does not automatically pay a refund or send a new billing email. Handle an exposed overpayment in Refund Requests, and check the revised invoice separately.

7. Review a reschedule request

Required permissions: Records access and reschedule approval.

  1. Open Records → Reschedule Requests.
  2. Find a Pending request by reference, guest or status.
  3. Review the old/new dates and the displayed additional charge.
  4. To accept, select Approve, read the confirmation and confirm the charge/date change.
  5. To decline, select Reject and enter the requested rejection reason.
  6. Read the result and check the refreshed request status.
  7. For an approval, verify the new dates in Reservations and the additional payable amount in Transactions.

Expected result: Approval updates the schedule and adds the notice fee plus any higher-date-rate adjustment to billing. A rejection leaves the original reservation schedule in place. The original booking remains occupied while the request is pending.

Notice eligibility and fees are based on the request's submission date, not the later approval date. Legacy requests submitted outside the notice period must be rejected and processed in the reservation editor as an authorized late exception.

Approval can fail if another booking/maintenance block conflicts or the requested start is too near/past. A lower-date rate does not automatically create a refund. Explain any additional balance to the guest; they can pay through Manage Booking when eligible.

8. Approve an ocular visit

Required permissions: Records access and ocular approval.

  1. Open Records → Ocular Visit Records.
  2. Find the Pending visit and review its reference, guest, date and slot.
  3. Confirm that the visit can be accommodated and has not started.
  4. Select Approve, confirm, then check the updated status.

Expected result: The visit is confirmed and the existing guest notification/email workflow is attempted. A viewing is free and does not create a paid stay reservation.

The current Records screen offers ocular approval, not an ocular rejection button. Guests can change or cancel eligible visits through their own Manage Booking screen. Contact the guest if other arrangements are needed.

11. Verify a guest GCash payment

Required permissions: Transactions access and payment approval.

  1. Open Transactions → Payment Verification Queue.
  2. Filter to Pending and select Review for the correct payment.
  3. Read the reservation reference, payment reference, amount, sender, payment type, receipt time and receipt-screening notes.
  4. Open the proof at full size. Check that it is a clear, complete original GCash screenshot.
  5. Independently check the resort's receiving GCash account. Match the actual transaction's amount, reference, recipient and date/time with the proof.
  6. For new guest receipts, select the required original-screenshot acknowledgement only after the image loads and you have checked it.
  7. Select Approve and then Confirm approval, or Reject and then Confirm rejection.
  8. Check the review result, reservation status, ledger balance and generated receipt.

Expected result: Approval credits the actual submitted payment and confirms an eligible initial reservation. Rejection of an initial payment can reject the reservation and release its dates. Rejection of a later balance payment leaves an already confirmed reservation active.

The receipt-reading check is not proof that money arrived. Reject photographed screens, altered receipts or inconsistencies rather than approving solely because the text was recognized. New guest receipts must use the current Philippine payment date; older/manual records can have no extracted receipt time and use their existing review workflow.

If a payment decision succeeds but an email/notification follow-up fails, refresh and inspect its saved status before retrying. Do not approve the same payment twice. If money arrived but a proof was rejected, reconcile the transfer with the guest before requesting another transfer.

12. Record a cash payment

Required permissions: Transactions access and manual payment entry.

This is the staff cash workflow. Online guest payment submission remains GCash only.

  1. Open Transactions > Transaction Ledger and find the reservation.
  2. Select Settle in cash on its row, or use Record cash and select the correct reservation.
  3. Check the reservation reference and outstanding balance. The form fills in the full balance; lower Cash applied to this bill if the guest is making a partial payment.
  4. Count the physical cash. If the guest tenders more than the bill, give change separately and record only the amount applied to the bill.
  5. Check I have counted and received this cash from the guest, then select Record payment and issue receipt.
  6. Read the result and remaining balance. Open Issued Receipts to view or print the receipt.

Expected result: The payment, balance, receipt and audit record are saved together. An initial payment on a pending reservation must cover the required 20% downpayment. A full settlement sets the outstanding balance to zero, including additional services already added to the bill. Cash is verified by the authorized staff member receiving it; no GCash screenshot or separate online-payment approval is needed.

The cash reference is generated automatically. If a save fails, retry in the same dialog with the same reference; a saved payment will not be recorded twice. If the balance changed, close the dialog, refresh the ledger and check the payment records before starting another entry. Pending payment/reschedule reviews and open refunds must be resolved first. Cancelled or completed reservations cannot receive new cash entries.

Administrators have cash-entry access. Other staff need the Record payments permission and Transactions access. Keep Cash active under Business Catalog > Payment methods; cash does not need a payment account or QR code.

13. Review and pay a refund

Cancel a reservation and preview its refund

  1. In Reservations, select the reservation from the calendar and choose Cancel reservation / review refund.
  2. Choose Guest request when staff is acting on the guest's request. Only an administrator may select Resort-attributable reason.
  3. Enter a documented cancellation reason and inspect the verified payments, prior refunds, initial deposit basis, deduction and refundable amount.
  4. A guest cancellation retains the initial 20% deposit: no refund for deposit-only bookings, the remaining 80% for fully paid bookings, and the verified amount above the deposit for partial payments. A resort-attributable cancellation refunds all remaining verified payments.
  5. Acknowledge the preview and confirm. No verified payment or no eligible amount produces Cancelled - No Refund. Eligible amounts create Pending refund review automatically, without a guest request.
  6. Read the success/alert message. Guest/finance in-app alerts are recorded with the cancellation. Guest email is attempted, including staff-entered guests with email addresses.

Pending payment proofs must be reviewed first. Staff handling a guest request can help outside the self-service notice window, while the deposit rule remains unchanged. The reservation stays Cancelled throughout refund processing. Do not collect the historical cancelled booking balance.

Review and approve

  1. Open Transactions > Refund Requests and select View / process.
  2. Check the verified payment records, references/proofs, previous refunds, policy snapshot and computed deduction. The amount is fixed; staff cannot type a replacement amount.
  3. The cashier enters review notes, checks the payment-record acknowledgement and selects Submit payment review.
  4. The request becomes Awaiting administrator approval. An active administrator selects Approve refund, or supplies a reason and selects Reject with reason.
  5. For an unprocessed legacy cancellation, an administrator first documents guest/resort responsibility and chooses Recalculate legacy cancellation refund. Any previous approval is reset for a fresh cashier review and recorded in the audit log. Completed refunds are preserved.

Review, payout and payout verification are separate configurable permissions. Approval/rejection is administrator-only. Module access alone grants no financial action rights.

Record and verify the payout

  1. Open the Approved - awaiting transfer request and recheck the approved amount and guest payout details.
  2. Send the money manually through GCash or another active approved payment method in Business Catalog. The website does not release money.
  3. Select the payout method, enter the real transfer reference and upload image/PDF evidence up to 8 MB.
  4. Select Submit transfer for verification. The status becomes Transfer submitted - awaiting verification; uploading proof does not mark the refund completed or reduce net paid totals.
  5. An authorized verifier opens the proof, matches amount, recipient and reference against provider transaction records, adds notes and checks the verification acknowledgement. An active administrator can perform this step for their own payout; other staff need a different verifier.
  6. Select Verify transfer and complete refund. Only this step sets Refunded and deducts the payout from net paid billing. An active administrator may review, approve, submit and verify using their own account; proof, reference, notes and explicit verification remain mandatory.

Every stage creates guest/finance in-app alerts and audit entries. Guest email is attempted; an email failure does not undo a saved action. References cannot be reused, one open request allocates funds per reservation, and stale/changed funds stop processing. Cancellation converts an existing excess request and resets review. An existing transfer awaiting verification must be verified before cancellation.

Expected result: Payments and issued receipts remain historical records. The verified refund updates net paid financial totals and refreshes the ledger. Cancelled balances are not shown as outstanding debt.

  2. Choose Line graph, Bar chart, Pie chart or Progress bars.

How to interpret the numbers

  • Sales counts money by payment verification date; older records without that date use submission date. Collections are before refunds, so they can differ from the ledger's net paid amount.
  • Guest reporting uses reservation creation dates, not arrival dates or guest registration dates. The table separately shows scheduled arrival. Headcount is booked adults plus children, including all reservation statuses.
  • First-time bookers have no reservation before the selected period; returning bookers have an earlier reservation. Each guest profile is counted once in those summary cards. These labels do not prove actual attendance.
  • Staff totals come from audit records, including separately described guest/system activity. Logged-action counts do not measure staff productivity.
  • Summary cards and charts cover the full date range. Table search/filters narrow the table, CSV and printed table; they do not change the summary totals.

  2. Choose Bookings, Collection forecast, Operations or Guest feedback.

TabMeaning
BookingsReservations created and their sources; includes all statuses, not occupancy or arrivals
Collection forecastActual verified collections plus a separately labelled estimate from historical averages
OperationsPayment submissions/pending reviews within the selected dates and logged activity
Guest feedbackAn on-demand AI summary of up to 100 latest approved reviews created within the dates

Collection forecast

The formula is actual gross collections ÷ selected calendar days × forecast days. Days with no payments are included. Forecast length is one day for Daily/Custom, seven days for Weekly and 30 days for Monthly. The forecast starts after the selected end date; a past range produces a historical estimate.

For example, PHP 28,000 collected across 28 selected days averages PHP 1,000 per day and estimates PHP 7,000 for the next seven days. The estimate is not money received, does not adjust for seasonality/holidays and has no statistical confidence score. Today's incomplete payments and limited history can affect it. Estimated new reservations use the same daily-average approach and include all statuses.

Collection charts contain actual amounts by date/payment type; estimates are not mixed into their pie total. Approval dates and gross/refund treatment match Sales Report.

Operations

Pending submissions count only payments submitted within the selected dates, not all outstanding payments. The payment-status pie compares payments with payments; the activity graph counts audit entries separately. Follow the task explanation to open Transactions or Audit Log for details.

Guest feedback

  1. Select Guest feedback, then Analyze feedback.
  2. Wait for the analysis and read the review sample size, sentiment percentages, summary and themes.
  3. Change chart style or export/print the result if needed.
  4. Use Analyze again to refresh the analysis.

These are AI-generated interpretations of the sampled reviews, not a survey of every guest. No approved reviews produces a no-data result. If analysis is unavailable, ask the administrator to check the service. Changing dates or refreshing analytics clears the previous analysis so results are not reused for the wrong period.
