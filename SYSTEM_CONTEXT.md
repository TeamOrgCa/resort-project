Management System (Admin-Facing)

Login Page - This page will allow authorized personnel to access the management system using their admin-assigned credentials. 

Dashboard - The dashboard will provide an overview of the key operational metrics which includes total bookings, upcoming reservations, occupancy status, daily revenue, and pending transactions. It may also display charts and summaries to allow the management to quickly assess the overall performance at a glance.

Reservation and Booking Management - This will enable the administrators to view and manage all reservation records that are made through both the website and walk-in bookings. It will include a calendar interface that displays the available dates, booked schedules, maintenance blocks, and public access schedules.

Reservation Confirmation and Approval - This module will allow staff to verify down payments and confirm the reservations within the system to finalize booking records.
Manual Booking Entry - Administrators will be able to encode walk-in and offline reservations into the system through this module for a centralized record-keeping.

Schedule Management - This is where administrators will be able to block certain dates for maintenance, private events, or other operational purposes to prevent any overlapping in bookings.

Transaction and Billing - This module acts as a point-of-sale system wherein the recording of payments is automated. It calculates totals, balances, and outstanding payments.
Invoice Generation - The system will automatically generate an invoice that details the breakdown of charges for availed reservations, services, and amenities.
Receipt Generation - This will automatically issue an official receipt as proof that the payment has been successful.

Administrative Reports - The administrative reports will generate automated reports that will assist management in monitoring operations and performance. These reports can be filtered by shift, day, week, or month.

Sales Report - Will provide a detailed overview of revenue generated by the resort within a specified period which can be filtered per day, week, or month. It will include the total revenue and a breakdown of income by service categories such as room accommodations, amenities, private bookings, public access, and additional services. This may also present graphs for comparisons across different time periods to easily visualize trends.

Financial Report - Will contain all monetary transactions such as received payments, outstanding balances, and adjustments. It will detail down payments, full payments, and pending payments for each reservation. This report will also summarize the total income, expenses, and cash flow of the business for specific periods.

Guest Report - Will compile detailed information about the guests who have made reservations. It will include booking history, number of guests per reservation and walk-in for dates open to public access, and provided guest contact information. It will also include demographic data such as frequent visitors and guest trends over time during the operations of the resort.

Staff Report - Will monitor all actions performed by authorized personnel within the system, including reservation confirmations, booking modifications, transaction processing, and schedule management. It will also track attendance and system login activity to ensure the accountability and transparency of the staff from MarVille Resort Complex.
Business Analytics and Sales Forecasting - This will utilize AI-enhanced tools that will analyze historical booking and sales data in order to identify trends, evaluate performance, and generate predictive forecasts to support managerial decision-making.

User Management and Access Control - This module will allow the administrators to manage and assign the roles and permissions of the system’s users to ensure that appropriate access levels are provided for managers and the staff.
Audit Log - This system feature will track and record all user activities within the system that will also provide a chronological record of all performed actions in the system with each entry containing the employee name, performed action, date and time, and the affected record (if applicable).
